I work where experimental design meets software.

The analysis is only as good as the design that came before it. My day-to-day runs from randomisation and plot layout through mixed-model fitting, multi-environment trial analysis and genomic prediction — then into the platforms that let breeders act on the result without needing a statistician in the room.

That software habit became a company. ResearchHive Analytics Ltd builds R Shiny platforms for research teams who have good data and no statistician. Separately, as co-founder and Research Manager of the Transport Aid Initiative, I've led field research on inclusive mobility and financial inclusion across Nigeria — VREF-, FCDO- and FIA Foundation-funded work, at one point coordinating a study team of over 300.

In 2026 I completed a CGIAR Knowledge Exchange placement at CIMMYT in Mexico, working on genomic prediction, tricot analysis and BioFlow — which is where GENOTwin started.

02Focus

Three problems I keep coming back to.

Breeding analytics

Multi-environment trials, genotype × environment interaction, genomic selection — and the tooling that makes those routine for a breeding programme rather than a specialist request.

Inclusive mobility

Who gets left out of urban transport, and what digital payment systems do to informal operators. Fieldwork in Ibadan, Benin City and Kampala.

Fairness in applied ML

Where prediction models for financial inclusion, transport demand and education quietly reproduce the inequality they were built to measure.
